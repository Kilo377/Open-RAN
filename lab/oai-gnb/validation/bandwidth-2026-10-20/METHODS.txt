Physical bandwidth sweep on OAI 2026.w42, source f80a88326801f5417405dea0b174879bac8940e0.
The official binary image is pinned by digest in progress.json. No source patches were applied.

Test order: 40 (baseline), 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 45, 50, 40 (repeat), then restore the original 40 MHz container.
Every requested 5 MHz step from 10 through 50 was attempted. Failed settings are N/A, never zero-throughput measurements.
The accepted container is stopped but retained; a separate trial container owns the B210 during the sweep.
An independent systemd recovery timer is armed before stopping the accepted baseline.
The original configuration is never edited. Recovery removes only the trial container and restarts the accepted container.

Radio: n78, 3.6192 GHz carrier center, SSB ARFCN 641280, 30 kHz SCS, 1 TX/1 RX, original TDD pattern.
TX attenuation setting is 12 dB and UHD-reported TX gain is 77.75 dB.
This fixes configured gain, not calibrated EIRP or power spectral density across bandwidths.
Derived configurations update Point A, DL/UL carrier PRBs and initial BWPs; CORESET0 fits within the carrier.
10 MHz uses full 15.36 MS/s sampling without -E. 50 MHz requests full 61.44 MS/s sampling.
Other trials use -E. Radio logs are retained to verify actual NR PRB count, center frequency, gain, sampling rate and analog filter.

The AGX requests a local HTTP stream from the existing OAI Link Monitor server.
Every data socket uses SO_BINDTODEVICE on the RM520N-GL USB network interface, with no Wi-Fi fallback.
Wi-Fi carries control reports only. The unchanged OAI 5GC carries the tested downlink through B210 and RM520N-GL.
Each 16 KiB frame has a per-run deterministic payload and sequence check; only fully validated frames count as received bytes.
Wait up to 90 s for at least 1 MiB verified module data, stop the probe, then measure a fresh 35 s stream.
Discard the first 5 s. Report the mean and sample standard deviation of three nominal 10 s windows.
Windows use approximately 0.5 s report intervals; they are time windows, not independent repeat experiments or confidence intervals.
The 40 MHz baseline is repeated at the end. A separate post-restoration check confirms fresh verified module data.
This is single-flow application goodput in this bench setup, not maximum physical-layer throughput.

Analysis requires successful complete payload verification, USB socket binding, sustained data, correct NR PRBs, center frequency and gain.
An analog filter wider than the NR channel does not make the OFDM channel wider (e.g. 10 MHz NR with a 20 MHz B210 filter).
A filter narrower than the requested channel is excluded because it may truncate active subcarriers.
The raw controller's exact-filter-equality flag is diagnostic only. Final verification uses filter coverage and actual NR PRBs.

35 and 45 MHz require a correction to the earlier planning advice: a generic OAI sampling case does not imply n78 UE support.
3GPP TS 38.101-1, Table 5.3.5-1, does not list 35/45 MHz for n78.
